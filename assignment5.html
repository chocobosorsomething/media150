Week 6: Color, Pattern, and Illustration

Homework 6: Illustrated Environment

For this assignment, I decided to go with the colors purple, orange, and green that represent the brightness of Filipino culture and my background growing up in the Philippines. Though I've lived here in New York since I was 8, I still have fond memories of my time in the Philippines, despite them becoming fainter as I age. I remember travelling to a province with my aunt back home, where I met my other cousins and distant relatives. Despite the tough life there, they managed to make it work with their shared community. I was particularly inspired remembering the living room of one of my cousins' homes that we visited. I used this color palette to show how bright and lively it was, remembering the number of people talking and relaxing on those couches. Especially the fan on the floor that barely did anything to soothe me with the intense heat radiating from the outside and the inside from the number of people; this piece and environment were meant to show the memories I had of being in the provinces of the Philippines as a child.
